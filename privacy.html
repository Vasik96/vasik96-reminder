Privacy Policy

Reminder stores your reminders on your device. If you choose to sign in with Google, the app saves a backup of your reminders in a hidden app-data folder in your own Google Drive, using the drive.appdata permission. The app cannot see any other files in your Drive.

The developer does not collect, store, or have access to your reminders, email address, or any other personal data. Nothing is shared with third parties.

You can disconnect Google Drive at any time in the app, and you can revoke access at myaccount.google.com under Security > Third-party access.
